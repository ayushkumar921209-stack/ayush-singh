More About Budget

Varanasi Itinerary

Here is a Varanasi (Banaras/Kashi) 4 Days 3 Nights budget plan for Friends, Family & Couple (per person approx.):

🧳 Budget Trip (Friends / Students)


Total: ₹4,500 – ₹7,000 per person

Expense Cost
Hotel/Hostel (3 nights) ₹900 – ₹2,000
Food (4 days) ₹1,200 – ₹1,800
Local Transport (Auto/E-rickshaw) ₹500 – ₹800
Ganga Boat Ride ₹200 – ₹500
Entry + Activities ₹500
Shopping/Extra ₹1,000
Total ₹4.5k–7k

Stay: Budget hotels near Assi Ghat / Dashashwamedh Ghat

👨‍👩‍👧 Family Comfortable Trip


Total: ₹8,000 – ₹15,000 per person

Expense Cost
Good Hotel (3 nights) ₹3,000 – ₹6,000
Food ₹2,000 – ₹3,500
Cab/Transport ₹1,500 – ₹2,500
Boat + Sightseeing ₹1,000
Shopping ₹2,000
Total ₹8k–15k

❤️ Couple Trip


Total: ₹10,000 – ₹20,000 for 2 people

Expense Cost
Couple Hotel (3 nights) ₹4,000 – ₹9,000
Food/Cafes ₹3,000 – ₹5,000
Transport ₹1,000 – ₹2,000
Private Boat Ride ₹1,000 – ₹2,500
Shopping + Photos ₹2,000
Total ₹10k–20k

🗓️ 4 Days Plan for all


Day 1 Check-in
Kashi Vishwanath Temple Darshan
Explore Ghats
Evening Ganga Aarti at Dashashwamedh Ghat
Day 2 Sunrise Boat Ride
Assi Ghat
Street food
Local markets
Day 3 Visit Sarnath
Temples
Shopping
Day 4 Morning walk
Breakfast
Return journey

Food to try: Banarasi Paan, Kachori Sabzi, Lassi, Chaat.

Best value estimate:


Friends ₹6k/person
Family ₹12k/person
Couple ₹15k total (comfortable)

(Travel cost to reach Varanasi not included.)
